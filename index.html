North Star Posture

Define the ideal posture and movement goals for this client

Release Movements

Target tight and overactive muscles that need to be released

Identified Problem Areas

Recommended Release Exercises

Upper Trapezius Stretch

2 sets x 30 sec hold

Gently tilt head to one side, place hand on opposite side of head and apply gentle pressure.

Pectoralis Doorway Stretch

2 sets x 45 sec hold

Stand in doorway, place forearms on frame, step forward to stretch chest muscles.

Hip Flexor Couch Stretch

2 sets x 60 sec hold

Place back foot on couch, front foot forward, push hips forward to stretch hip flexors.

Reset Movements

Activate and re-educate inhibited muscles to restore proper movement patterns

Activation Targets

Based on assessment findings, these muscles likely need activation and re-education:

  • Deep neck flexors (for forward head posture)
  • Lower trapezius and serratus anterior (for shoulder elevation)
  • Glutes and deep core stabilizers (for postural stability)

Recommended Reset Exercises

Deep Neck Flexor Activation (Chin Tucks)

3 sets x 10 reps, 5 sec hold

Lying down, gently draw chin back creating a double chin. Feel stretch at base of skull.

Wall Angels (Scapular Activation)

3 sets x 15 reps

Back to wall, arms in goal post position, slide arms up and down maintaining wall contact.

Glute Bridge (Glute Activation)

3 sets x 12 reps, 3 sec hold

Lying on back, knees bent, lift hips by squeezing glutes. Focus on glute activation.

Dead Bug (Core Stabilization)

3 sets x 8 each side

Lying on back, arms up, knees bent 90°. Lower opposite arm and leg slowly, maintain neutral spine.

Rebuild Program

Strengthen and build endurance in key postural muscles for long-term stability

Strengthening Phase Goals

Progressive strengthening to build postural endurance and stability:

  • Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4): Basic strength and motor control
  • Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8): Progressive loading and endurance
  • Phase 3 (Weeks 9-12): Functional integration

Recommended Strengthening Exercises

Resistance Band Rows

3 sets x 12-15 reps

Seated or standing, pull band to chest focusing on squeezing shoulder blades together.

Progression: Light → Medium → Heavy resistance

Bird Dog (Dynamic Stability)

3 sets x 10 each side, 10 sec hold

On hands and knees, extend opposite arm and leg, hold position with stable spine.

Progression: Static hold → Dynamic movement → Added resistance

Single-Leg Deadlift

3 sets x 8 each leg

Balance on one leg, hinge at hip keeping spine neutral, reach toward ground.

Progression: Bodyweight → Light weight → Progressive loading

Pallof Press (Anti-Rotation)

3 sets x 10 each side

Hold band at chest, press out and resist rotation, maintaining stable core.

Progression: Static hold → Dynamic press → Increased resistance
